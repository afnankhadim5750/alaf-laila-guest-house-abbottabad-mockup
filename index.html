Welcome

A guest house, not a hotel lobby.

Alaf Laila has been taking in travellers since 2013 — families driving up from Islamabad, visitors on their way to the Galyat hills, and Abbottabad regulars who want a quiet room at the end of the day. There is no chain behind it, no booking engine of its own: you ring the house, and the house answers.

The building sits in Kaghan Colony, opposite Usmania Restaurant on the Abbottabad–Mansehra Road in Mandian — close enough to town for dinner, far enough out that the air is cooler and the hills are in view. Inside: a garden, a shared lounge, a terrace, and fifteen air-conditioned rooms with private balconies.

  • Kaghan Colony, Mandian — on the Abbottabad–Mansehra Road
  • Fifteen rooms — doubles, twins and a single
  • Garden, shared lounge and terrace
  • Free WiFi throughout, free self parking

Garden & terrace

Outside is half the stay.

The garden is where the house slows down: morning tea on the terrace, an evening by the outdoor fireplace, and the hills in view from the balconies. Abbottabad sits at the foot of the Galyat hills, and the road from here climbs straight into pine forest.

Dining

Eat in, or take it with you.

There is a restaurant in the house, room service to the rooms, and packed lunches on request for early starts into the hills. A shared kitchen — fridge, microwave and toaster — is there for guests who prefer to make their own.

  • On-site restaurant with table service
  • Room service (limited hours)
  • Packed lunches on request
  • Shared kitchen for guests

Please note: breakfast is not served at the house. Ask at the front desk and the team will point you to the nearest options on Mansehra Road.

Shared spaces

Rooms are private. The house is shared.

A shared lounge with a television, a second sitting area by the fire, and the garden outside — the parts of a guest house that make it feel like a house, not a corridor of doors.

For families

Children welcome. Properly welcome.

Family rooms sleep four, children of all ages are welcome, and there are indoor and outdoor play areas for them — plus childcare listed at the house. It is a practical base for a family trip to the hills: parking is free, the garden gives children somewhere to run, and the rooms are soundproofed.

  • Family rooms that sleep four
  • Indoor & outdoor play areas
  • Childcare listed at the house
  • Free self parking on site

Good to know

Questions, answered plainly.

How do I book a room?

By phone or WhatsApp — the house has no booking engine of its own. Call 0345 9566871 for tonight’s rate and availability. The house is also listed on Booking.com and Trip.com.

Is breakfast included?

No — breakfast is not served at the house. There is an on-site restaurant, room service and packed lunches on request, and the front desk can point you to options on Mansehra Road.

Is there parking and WiFi?

Yes to both: free self parking on site, and free WiFi throughout the property, in the rooms and the shared spaces.

Can families with children stay?

Yes. There are family rooms that sleep four, children of all ages are welcome, and the house has indoor and outdoor play areas plus childcare listed.

What time is check-in and check-out?

As listed on the booking pages: check-in from 10:00 am, check-out by 2:00 pm. Please confirm when you call, as the times shown can differ between listings.

Is there a pool?

No — the house does not have a pool. It does have a garden, a terrace, a shared lounge and an outdoor fireplace.
